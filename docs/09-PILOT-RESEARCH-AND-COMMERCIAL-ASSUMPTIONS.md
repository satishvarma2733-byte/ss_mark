# Simply Sit pilot research and commercial assumptions

Research date 25 October 2026. The owner confirmed a broad hospitality marketplace and simultaneous website plus Android/iOS release, and requested researched recommendations for city, budget and timing. Recommend Bengaluru for validation, initially one compact Koramangala cluster, with a 22-week staffed build and a planning envelope of INR 27-42 lakh. These recommendations use a transparent model; they are not signed budgets, supplier quotations or proof of demand.

## City recommendation

Colliers reported on 28 September 2026 that Bengaluru continued to lead office demand, with average quarterly uptake around 5 million square feet since 2025. Its report identifies flexible-space operators as a significant contributor to Indian office demand. This signals a professional customer base and established flexible-space alternatives, but does not measure hourly hospitality bookings. [Colliers primary research](https://www.colliers.com/en-in/news/press-release-india-office-market-q3-2026).

JLL's 31 August 2026 report puts Bengaluru at 24.7% of India's H1 2026 office gross leasing. This supports testing the city while remaining an indirect demand indicator. [JLL India Office Market Dynamics Q2 2026](https://www.jll.com/en-in/insights/market-dynamics/india-office).

Start recruitment in a roughly 3 km Koramangala cluster. The exact service polygon must follow committed supply and operational checks. Koramangala is a fieldwork hypothesis, not a measured neighbourhood ranking. No desk research here verifies idle tables, outside-food acceptance or venue willingness in that neighbourhood. Indiranagar is a separate fallback cluster if recruitment fails; do not split the first pilot across distant areas.

| Candidate | Evidence and limitation | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bengaluru | Current primary office/flex demand signals; willingness to pay unproven | First validation city |
| Pune | JLL reports substantial office activity; venue access and local operations unknown | Alternative if founder access is stronger |
| Hyderabad | JLL reports strong absorption; same supply-validation gap | Alternative if partner economics are stronger |

Founder access can outweigh city-level statistics. Five committed venues in another city may be more useful than a promising market without an operating owner. Compare actual supply before spending on a new city.

## Two-week validation before major spend

Interview 20 venue decision makers and 30 prospective customers. Recruit across suitable cafés, restaurants, hotel lounges and hospitality spaces. Ask about idle windows, existing reservations, minimum spend, food/delivery rules, cleaning, Wi-Fi/power, access restrictions and authority over inventory. Brewery spaces need case-specific review of alcohol, age and premises rules before listing; the app does not sell alcohol.

Obtain at least five expressions of interest with named resources, weekly windows, price/margin expectations, a staff owner and food rules. Observe actual spare capacity. Do not sell an entire floor alongside its contained tables.

Test a clickable web/native prototype with ten customers and three venue staff. Indicative whole-table hourly prices of INR 100, 150 and 200 for a defined two-person table are experiment inputs, not researched market prices. Record the customer's current workaround and willingness to return without discounts. Optional food stays separate from booking price.

Run a limited concierge test under the owner's approved collection/refund arrangement: target 20 completed reservations, at least two repeat customers and full reconciliation. This review has not contacted venues or taken payments. Proceed only if five venues provide useful supply, a repeated customer use case is observed and unit economics work. Revise segment, price or supply if evidence fails.

## Native account and payment considerations

Apple lists its Developer Program at USD 99 annually or local currency where available; actual organization enrollment depends on legal-entity verification. [Apple membership](https://developer.apple.com/programs/whats-included/), [organization enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/).

Expo recommends development builds for production native development and supports EAS cloud compilation, including iOS builds from non-macOS development machines. Native payment SDKs and link-return behavior need these builds; an Expo Go preview is insufficient proof. [Expo development builds](https://docs.expo.dev/develop/development-builds/introduction/).

Apple's guidelines distinguish services consumed outside the app from digital purchases. Physical venue booking appears to fit that category; document this interpretation for review and recheck before submission. App functionality and reviewer access also matter; a bare website wrapper is a release risk. [Apple review guidelines, sections 3.1.3 and 4.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/). Check Google Play organization verification, payment policy and applicable testing requirements against the actual account; do not assume a universal testing period.

## Commercial hypotheses and economics

Test a 20% commission on the agreed commissionable booking subtotal, no customer service fee, and weekly reporting for completed reconciled partner payables. Actual money transfer timing must follow the approved provider arrangement. Commission base, tax responsibilities, provider fees and cancellation rules need signed terms; code cannot establish them.

Illustration only: two hours at INR 150/hour yields INR 300 before tax. A 20% commission yields INR 60 platform gross and INR 240 partner pre-adjustment share. Modelling payment expense at 2% of gross (INR 6), support at INR 10 and other variable cost at INR 1 leaves INR 43 contribution before tax effects, discounts, refunds, chargebacks and acquisition. The 2% is a model input, not a provider quote. A refund can reverse that margin. Do not count partner funds as platform revenue; confirm accounting/tax treatment professionally against final contracts.

Measure paid hours, bookable-hours denominator, net commission, discount funder, actual provider/support expense, refunds, chargebacks, repeat usage, venue retention and voluntarily reported extra venue spend. Do not forecast profitability from office demand or assumed high occupancy.
